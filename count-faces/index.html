Game 6

Count the Happy Faces

A crowd appears. Count only the open, smiling faces. Angry, sad, startled and blank do not count.

The faster you answer, the more you earn: an exact count is worth up to 150, dropping to 50 as the bar runs down. One off earns a third of that. The crowd is drawn fresh every time, so nobody can memorise the answer.

How many happy faces?
