How the boards work

Every game keeps its own leaderboard. Points come first, and a tie goes to whoever finished faster. The clock pauses while an answer is on screen and stops before the name box appears, so take your time reading and spelling. Your name is remembered on this device for the next game. Any board can be refreshed to pull in attempts made since you opened it. Putting a board on a big screen? Open the display boards.
